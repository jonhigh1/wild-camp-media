## Brand Snapshot

- **Company:** Wild Camp Media (WCM)
- **Canvas:** Carbon black #000000 (logo ground)
- **Primary ink:** Warm cream #F5EFE0
- **Accent:** Burnt orange #D35400 (secondary sun #E67E22)
- **Punctuation:** REC red #C0392B (dot + label only)
- **Fonts (logo):** Distressed uppercase sans ("WILD CAMP"), clean condensed sans ("MEDIA")
- **Tone:** Rugged, authentic, adventurous, professional
- **Core message:** One brand, four services — DP/videographer, drone (FAA Part 107), jobsite video, events — documentary-grade visuals for blue-collar and craft industries.

## Logo analysis

Camera viewfinder framing a mountain scene: cream peaks, dark pines, orange
sun, star-specked night sky, red REC indicator top-left. Distressed texture on
the wordmark reads screen-printed, vintage outdoors. High contrast, near-black
ground. The mark is simultaneously "camera" and "wild" — the whole brand in
one glyph.

Implications for the site:
- Dark hero is native to the brand (logo ground is black).
- Cream (not pure white) is the brand ink — matches Creative Giants' bone
  white rule by coincidence and by logo.
- Orange is the working accent; red stays punctuation (REC/active states
  only), exactly how Freshman rations its signal red.
- Distressed wordmark can appear on the cream body in black; keep logo mark
  full-color only on dark surfaces.

## Outline digest (from WCM Website Outline v1.pdf)

Goal: one brand, four service areas. One page per service, no subpages, no
separate About/Portfolio/Contact pages — each service page carries its own
work, bio, and contact. Consistent branding throughout.

- **Home:** intro, four services, strong work examples, brief bio, contact invitation.
- **DP/Videographer Services:** audience = producers/agencies/production companies/brands/directors/orgs. Message: experienced, dependable camera + cinematography support. Reel, client list, FAA 107, services (DP, camera op, interviews/docs, commercial/branded, corporate, lighting, small-crew, add'l camera, equipment packages, pre-pro, drone), working style (professional, prepared, collaborative, adaptable, reliable), bio, selected work, testimonials/logos, contact form, CTA **Check Availability**.
- **Drone Photo/Video:** aerial for TV/corporate/commercial/jobsite. Reel, project types, FAA 107, service area + travel, safety/planning, contact form.
- **Jobsite Video Services:** audience = construction/contractors/developers/architects/trades. Message: document the work, help companies market themselves. Benefits (showcase quality, trust, progress docs, culture, recruiting, content library). Services (one-time/recurring visits, progress docs, recap videos, company overviews, interviews, social clips, website video, photography, drone, before/after, recruiting). 5-step process. Engagements (one-time visit, progress series, project showcase, monthly content, company story). Jobsite examples, construction-focused bio, 9 FAQs, contact form, CTA **Discuss Your Jobsite**.
- **Events:** WCM-hosted events (videography workshops, listening club, screenings). Upcoming/past, host bio, updates signup.

## Design system targets (chosen by Jon)

- **Hero:** Freshman (styles.refero.design/style/a6284fcd) — near-black canvas, white/cream type, ultralight display weight, hairline dividers, project ticker, one red accent.
- **Body:** Creative Giants (styles.refero.design/style/ff8f39ee) — bone-white cream canvas (#fffef7 family), weight-300 display 54–84px with tight tracking, sharp-corner editorial documentary photography, chromatic accents rationed, no shadows, pill buttons only on interactive elements.
- **Transition:** hard cut, dark → cream. A cut, not a fade — cinema language.
